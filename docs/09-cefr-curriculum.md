# 09 — CEFR Curriculum Structure

**Status:** Draft for review · Authored by the CEFR German Specialist role; targets align with Goethe/telc/*Profile Deutsch* practice.

---

## 1. Level architecture

Each CEFR level splits into **2 sub-levels** (A1.1, A1.2, …), each sub-level into **4–6 thematic units**, each unit into **3–6 lesson templates** (1–3 concepts each) + 1 checkpoint.

| Level | Sub-levels | Units | Concepts (approx.) | New vocab | Cumulative vocab | Est. hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | A1.1, A1.2 | 10 | 70 | 700 | 700 | 80–100 |
| A2 | A2.1, A2.2 | 10 | 85 | 800 | 1 500 | 100–120 |
| B1 | B1.1, B1.2 | 11 | 100 | 1 000 | 2 500 | 150–180 |
| B2 | B2.1, B2.2 | 11 | 110 | 1 500 | 4 000 | 180–220 |
| C1 | C1.1, C1.2 | 10 | 90 | 2 000+ | 6 000+ | 220–280 |

*(Hours = guided study; planner recalibrates per learner from observed pace.)*

## 2. Per-level definition template

Every level is defined by the same 10 sections (stored in curriculum tables):

1. **Grammar topics** (concept nodes) 2. **Vocabulary goals** (frequency- and topic-based packs)
3. **Reading objectives** 4. **Listening objectives** 5. **Writing objectives** 6. **Speaking objectives**
7. **Communication skills** (functional can-dos) 8. **Culture notes** 9. **Assessment criteria** 10. **Exit requirements**

## 3. Level content summaries

### A1 — Breakthrough
- **Grammar:** articles & gender; personal pronouns; Präsens regular/irregular; sein/haben; V2 word order; yes/no & W-questions; negation nicht/kein; Akkusativ; possessives; modal verbs (können, möchten, müssen); separable verbs (intro); imperative (Sie); Perfekt (receptive intro A1.2).
- **Vocabulary:** 700 highest-frequency words — greetings, family, numbers, time, food, shopping, home, city, daily routine.
- **Reading:** signs, forms, short messages. **Listening:** slow clear speech, times/prices, simple directions.
- **Writing:** forms, postcards, 2–4 sentence messages. **Speaking:** introduce self, order, ask price, tell time.
- **Communication:** greeting, introducing, shopping basics, café ordering. **Culture:** du/Sie, greetings by region.
- **Exit:** graph coverage ≥ 90 %; vocab ≥ 630 known; can write 30-word message; 3-min guided dialogue; checkpoint average ≥ 75 %.

### A2 — Waystage
- **Grammar:** Dativ (articles, pronouns, prepositions); Wechselpräpositionen; Perfekt (full); Präteritum haben/sein/modals; separable/inseparable verbs; reflexive verbs; comparative/superlative; subordinate clauses weil/dass/wenn; adjective endings (Nom/Akk); imperative (du/ihr); future with werden (intro).
- **Vocabulary:** +800 — work, health, travel, weather, clothing, celebrations, media, feelings.
- **Reading:** short articles, emails, ads. **Listening:** announcements, simple dialogues at natural-ish pace.
- **Writing:** informal emails (50–80 words), simple narration in Perfekt. **Speaking:** past events, appointments, doctor visits, invitations.
- **Culture:** Termine & punctuality, Anmeldung/bureaucracy basics, festivals.
- **Exit:** vocab ≥ 1 350; 80-word email; 5-min scenario (doctor/shopping) with task completion; all A2 checkpoints passed.

### B1 — Threshold
- **Grammar:** all Dativ/Genitiv usage; adjective endings complete; Präteritum (written narration); Plusquamperfekt; relative clauses (Nom/Akk/Dat); infinitive with zu; Konjunktiv II (würde + common forms: hätte/wäre/könnte); passive Präsens/Präteritum; temporal clauses (als/während/bevor/nachdem); indirect questions; verbs with prepositions.
- **Vocabulary:** +1 000 — opinion & argument, education, environment, technology, relationships, news.
- **Reading:** newspaper articles, blog posts, short stories. **Listening:** radio reports, longer conversations, main + detail.
- **Writing:** structured opinion text (150 words), formal email/complaint. **Speaking:** narrate, express & justify opinion, handle complications (lost luggage, rebooking).
- **Culture:** work culture, school system, regional variety awareness.
- **Exit:** vocab ≥ 2 250; 150-word argued text scoring ≥ 3.5/5 average rubric; 8-min interview scenario; B1 exam blueprint pass ≥ 70 % per skill.

### B2 — Vantage
- **Grammar:** passive all tenses + with modals; Konjunktiv II complete (past, passive); Partizipialattribute; nominalization ↔ verbal style; two-part conjunctions (je…desto, sowohl…als auch); subjective modal meanings (er soll/dürfte…); Genitiv prepositions; extended attributes; discourse connectors.
- **Vocabulary:** +1 500 — abstract concepts, professional fields, collocations, common idioms, Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen (basic set).
- **Reading:** editorials, professional texts, literature excerpts. **Listening:** discussions, lectures, films (standard speech).
- **Writing:** essay/report (250 words), formal correspondence, describing graphs. **Speaking:** debate, present pros/cons fluently, workplace meetings.
- **Culture:** political system, media landscape, workplace norms.
- **Exit:** vocab ≥ 3 600; 250-word essay ≥ 3.5/5; 10-min debate holding position; error rate in free production below B2 band threshold.

### C1 — Effective Operational Proficiency
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv I & reported speech; complex hypotaxis; stylistic passive alternatives (lassen sich, -bar, man); functional verb structures (Funktionsverbgefüge); modal particles mastery (doch, ja, eben, halt…); register switching; text cohesion devices; scientific/formal style.
- **Vocabulary:** +2 000 — nuance, register variants, idiomatic mastery, academic word list.
- **Reading:** academic texts, contracts, literature. **Listening:** fast native speech, lectures, regional coloring.
- **Writing:** academic essay (350+ words), abstracts, formal reports with register control. **Speaking:** nuanced argument, presentations, negotiation, humor/irony comprehension.
- **Culture:** DACH differences, academic conventions, professional etiquette depth.
- **Exit:** vocab ≥ 5 600; C1 exam blueprint (all skills ≥ level minimum); sustained 15-min discussion; writing shows register control (rubric ≥ 4/5 on style).

## 4. Assessment criteria (uniform machinery, level-specific bands)

Per skill and level, blueprints define: item counts, difficulty ranges (theta bands), pass percentages, and — for production — rubric descriptors per score 0–5 mapped to CEFR can-do statements. Continuous assessment (observations) does the day-to-day estimation; blueprints gate promotions only.

## 5. Content production plan

| Wave | Content | Source strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1 complete (concepts, vocab pack, 10 scenarios, blueprints) | Hand-authored by linguist + reviewed AI-drafted examples |
| 2 | A2 complete | Same pipeline, tooling from wave 1 |
| 3 | B1 → B2 → C1 | AI-assisted drafting at scale, 100 % human review before publish |

**Rule:** AI may *draft* curriculum content offline in the authoring pipeline; nothing enters a published curriculum version without human approval. This is different from runtime generation (exercises/dialogues), which is constrained-and-validated but not human-reviewed.

Vocabulary source: frequency lists cross-checked against Goethe-Zertifikat wordlists (licensing audit required — `NFR-CMP-2`).
